Technical Specs

Everything you need to know.

Compatibility, real performance numbers, privacy details, and system requirements.

What DiskCleaner Scans

DiskCleaner scans specific known paths on your startup disk — not the entire volume. It also shows disk usage for connected external drives.

Startup Disk — 7 Categories
  • App Cache — ~/Library/Caches
  • Browser Cache — per-browser paths
  • Screenshots — ~/Desktop & ~/Pictures
  • macOS Trash — ~/.Trash
  • Developer Data — Xcode, Simulator, VS Code, JetBrains
  • App Leftovers — ~/Library/Application Support
  • External Storage — disk usage overview only
Not Scanned
  • Full disk enumeration
  • Network drives or NAS
  • Time Machine volumes
  • Optical or removable media

Scan Speed

Scans specific folder paths in parallel using Swift concurrency — not full disk enumeration. Speed depends on how large your caches and DerivedData are, not the filesystem format.

Quick Scan — all 7 categories, depth 3< 10s
Deep Scan — small DerivedData10–20s
Deep Scan — large DerivedData (~20GB)20–45s
Deep Scan — very large caches (50GB+)45–90s

Tested on Apple Silicon SSD. APFS vs HFS+ has negligible effect since we target known paths, not full volume traversal.
